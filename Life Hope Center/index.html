Our History

Rooted in a century
of mission

The concept of the Life Hope Center is rooted in the long-standing mission of the Seventh-day Adventist Church, which began in the mid-1800s with a strong focus on health, education, and gospel ministry. From its early years, the church established sanitariums, clinics, schools, and publishing houses to serve both physical and spiritual needs.

In more recent decades, the church recognized the need for more accessible, community-level outreach centers — ministries that go beyond church buildings and address urban poverty, health crises, and social needs. In the Philippines, Life Hope Centers became part of the church's strategy for Total Member Involvement (TMI) and urban mission.

"Mingling with people, showing sympathy, ministering to needs, winning confidence, and inviting them to follow Him."
